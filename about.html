Our Purpose

Learn • Lead • Serve • Grow

Sahyog is a platform where individuals discover their potential, develop their skills, and create a meaningful impact on society. Through active participation in social initiatives, members build leadership, gain real-world skills, and contribute towards building stronger and more empowered communities.

At Sahyog, our purpose is simple yet powerful: to empower women with dignity and livelihood, to educate children with hope and opportunity, and to enable youth to become changemakers of tomorrow.

Why Sahyog?

  • Because real change begins when youth take responsibility.
  • Because empowerment is not charity — it is opportunity.
  • Because education is the strongest foundation for a better future.

Our Story

The Journey of Sahyog

From a small initiative to a growing movement of purpose, service, and change.

1

The Beginning

A small initiative driven by the belief that real change begins at the grassroots level.

2

Community Engagement

Initiated local outreach programs and built strong connections with communities.

3

Structured Growth

Launched core initiatives focusing on education, skill development, and awareness.

4

Expanding Impact

Growing into a structured organization creating measurable social impact.

SAHYOG began as a free study center initiated by Yogesh Sir, starting with just 4 students with a simple belief that education can transform lives. Within just three months, the initiative expanded from 4 to 400 students, reflecting the growing trust, participation, and community support. When COVID brought difficult times, Yogesh Sir and the team stepped forward to serve society selflessly, standing with communities when support mattered the most. Through these experiences, the team recognized the immense power of youth and decided to channel their efforts into a more organized, structured, and impact-driven movement. What began as informal community efforts evolved into a legally structured, youth-led initiative — expanding its reach, strengthening its impact, and creating meaningful change in society.
